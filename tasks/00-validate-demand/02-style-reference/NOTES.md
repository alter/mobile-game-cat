# The set, 2026-08-26

31 App Store screenshots supplied by the owner, five titles, in
`screenshots/<title>/`. Originals were 38 MB at store resolution (1242 px
wide); stored here downscaled to 900×1400, quality 86 — 6.3 MB, which is more
than enough to judge palette, shape language and how a screen reads at a
glance, and small enough to live in the repository. The originals stay outside
git, under `tmp/`.

`contact-sheet.jpg` puts all 31 on one page, grouped by title. That is the
sheet to look at for VERIFY 2 — "does this read as one visual family" is a
question about the set, and it cannot be answered by opening files one at a
time.

**Item 2 — human eye: not an agent's call.** ROLES.md is explicit that an agent
reports a result looks good almost regardless of the result, and this is one of
the three places where an outsider must judge. The contact sheet exists to make
that judgement cheap; the judgement itself is the owner's.

## One observation offered as material, not as a verdict

Looking at the sheet, the five titles do not form one family, and that is
useful rather than a problem. Merge Mansion is dark, painterly and
photo-realistic; Royal Match and Homescapes are bright, high-saturation,
heavy-outlined; Travel Town and Gossip Harbor sit between, with flatter shapes
and a warmer mid-tone palette. The style canon in `art-brief.md` section 3
calls for soft rounded shapes and a warm muted palette — the closest
comparables on this sheet are Travel Town's item trays and Gossip Harbor's
room interiors, and the furthest is Merge Mansion. That is worth settling
before `03-art-prompt-props` writes a prompt, because "judged against real
comparables" only works if it is clear which comparables.
