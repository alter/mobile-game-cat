TASK: One cat, two states, for the creatives

GOAL
  Prove the same cat can be shown readably worse and readably better before
  $300 of ad spend is committed. This is the project's single largest art
  risk, pulled forward from 40-art so it is learned for free instead of after
  the money moves.

CONTEXT
  cat-shelter-mvp.md section 10 (cat is a desaturated base + masks, not parts)
  art-brief.md section 7 (full cat spec, why it's riskiest, two-attempt-
    then-hire-an-artist rule)
  art-prompts.md section 4 (state table, "same cat not two cats" wording)

SCOPE
  + One cat, two states only (worst and best of the three-state arc in
    art-brief.md section 7) - thin, matted, hunched vs. sleek, relaxed.
  + Same short-haired base image edited/re-prompted between states, not two
    independent generations - continuity is the entire point.
  - Not the third state, long-haired variant, or six-silhouette layer/mask
    system - that is the full 4.4/4.5 in 40-art, after "go".
  - Not colour recolouring by shader - these are flat generated images for
    the creative, not the in-game palette system.

OUTCOME
  Two cat images (worse, better) that a stranger looking at both agrees is
  one cat, not two.

VERIFY (HUMAN)
  1. Outside person, shown both images without context, answers "same cat or
     two cats?" - "same cat" required to pass.
  2. Outside person independently confirms which of the two looks improved.

ROLE
  ART, HUMAN

DEPENDS
  00-validate-demand/03-art-prompt-props
