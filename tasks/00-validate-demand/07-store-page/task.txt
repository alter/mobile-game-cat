TASK: Store placeholder page

GOAL
  Give the creatives a landing page that accepts traffic and records intent,
  since Apple does not support publishing a real App Store listing without a
  build that has passed review.

CONTEXT
  knowledge/analytics/02-benchmarks-and-attribution.md section 'The "store
    page stub" as a separate technique' (why a real App Store listing
    isn't available pre-build, and what the fake-door alternative is)

SCOPE
  + A standalone web page imitating an app store card (icon, name, 2-3
    screenshots or the creative itself, an "Install" button).
  + Click on "Install" recorded as a proxy conversion, with source/campaign
    tagged per creative.
  - Not a real App Store listing - Apple requires a build that has passed
    review before any listing, real or pre-order, can go live; that build
    does not exist yet.
  - Not Custom Product Pages or Product Page Optimization - both require an
    already-published app and are for comparing creatives after launch, not
    before one exists.

OUTCOME
  A live URL that records a click-through per creative/campaign.

VERIFY (NATIVE)
  1. A test click from each creative's campaign link produces a recorded
     entry with the correct source tag.
  2. Page loads on a real mobile browser, not just desktop.

ROLE
  NATIVE

DEPENDS
  none
