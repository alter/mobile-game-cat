This task was previously marked P0 with "longest lead time, blocks the end
of M6." That was wrong, and dropping the blocking claim removes a stall
point. Apple: "By joining the Apple Developer Program, you can distribute
free apps on the App Store under the Apple Developer Program License
Agreement," while "To sell your apps on the App Store or offer In-App
Purchases, the Account Holder must sign the Paid Apps Agreement." This MVP
is free and takes no money - the "+3 slots" button on the lose screen is a
stub by design (see `cat-shelter-mvp.md` section 6). So: no Paid Apps
Agreement, no tax forms, no bank details are required to ship the MVP.

Start it anyway if idle time allows, since bank/tax processing is slow
(Apple's own material notes 24 hours nominal, with delays of several weeks
reported on developer forums - see `knowledge/ios/01-appstore-requirements-2026.md`)
and it will be needed the day real monetisation appears. But it is off the
critical path, and this task tree should not pretend otherwise.

Moves toward the goal: no, it is admission. But the goal is unreachable
without it eventually.
