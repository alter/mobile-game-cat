TASK: Outcomes - win and jam

GOAL
  Two distinguishable endings. Win when the pile is empty, jam when the shelf is
  full with no match available.

CONTEXT
  cat-shelter-mvp.md section 3
  reviews/2026-08-24-refactor-difficulty.md (why the move limit was removed)

SCOPE
  + GameOutcome.Win, GameOutcome.ShelfJammed.
  + Win is checked BEFORE jam. Note what this does and does not buy: the two
    can never contend for the same move, because a pile carries every kind in
    multiples of three and TryMatch removes each triple as it forms, so the
    shelf is empty exactly when the pile is. The ordering is correct and also
    vacuous. What is reachable, and what the tests pin, is the near miss - a
    final placement into the shelf's last free slot that matches on the way in.
  - No OutOfMoves. A move takes exactly one item, winning needs exactly as many
    moves as there are items, so any limit is either unreachable or makes the
    level unwinnable.

OUTCOME
  Board reports one of two outcomes; the ordering bug cannot recur.

VERIFY (ARCH)
  1. One unit test per outcome.
  2. A test for the reachable boundary between the two: the final placement
     takes the shelf's LAST free slot and completes a triple on the way in -
     result is Win, not a jam.
  3. A test pinning why the two outcomes can never compete for one move: at a
     win the shelf is empty. If a future rule lets a pile hold a kind in
     non-triples, this test fails and the ordering becomes a live question.

  Item 2 was reworded and item 3 added, 27.08.2026. Item 2 used to read "last
  item empties the pile AND fills the shelf - result is Win", which VERIFY.md
  proved unsatisfiable: the state cannot occur and the branch it aimed at
  (Board.cs:120-130) is unreachable. The old verdict stands as written - it is
  the criterion that was wrong, not the verification.
