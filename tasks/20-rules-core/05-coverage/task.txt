TASK: Core coverage

GOAL
  Line coverage on Core at 90% or above, with every termination branch reached.

CONTEXT
  knowledge/unity/04-test-framework.md (coverage from the command line; the
  package has no built-in threshold, enforcement is a separate CI step)

SCOPE
  + Coverage measured and reported.
  + A CI step that fails the build below the threshold.
  - Not a coverage target for View or Tools.

OUTCOME
  Coverage report produced by command, threshold enforced by the build.

VERIFY (ARCH)
  1. Coverage report shows line rate at or above 90% on Core.
  2. Lowering the threshold in CI and reverting proves the step actually fails.

ROLE
  CORE, QA

DEPENDS
  20-rules-core/04-outcomes
