TASK: Solver - solvable, in how many moves

GOAL
  Answer, for one level, whether it is solvable at all and how many moves a
  sensible policy needs, fast enough to gate generation.

CONTEXT
  tools/solver/solver.py (DFS with failure memoization, sensible-play heuristic)
  tools/solver/rules.py (Outcome, replay - what "solved" means)
  tools/tests/test_solver.py
  knowledge/solver/01-tile-match-solver.md (why full search does not scale,
    the memoization approach)

SCOPE
  + `solve(level)` returns a move sequence or None within a time deadline.
  + Failure-state memoization to prune repeated dead branches.
  - Not a difficulty measure - solvability only. Win-rate measurement is
    04-difficulty-curve and, later, 10-remeasure-curve-partial-info.
  - Not used as a difficulty oracle once hidden kinds land - see
    09-hidden-kinds/NOTES.md.

OUTCOME
  `solve()` answers under 2 seconds per level on known-solvable and
  known-dead-end fixtures.

VERIFY (ARCH)
  1. `pytest tools/tests/test_solver.py -v` green.
  2. Five known-solvable and five known-dead-end fixtures both resolve
     correctly (test_five_known_solvable, test_five_known_dead_ends).
  3. Manual timing: `solve()` on a 60-item level completes under 2s.

ROLE
  TOOLS, ARCH (review)

DEPENDS
