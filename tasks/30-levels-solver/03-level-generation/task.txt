TASK: Level generation

GOAL
  Generate a batch of levels by rule - layered pile, kind counts divisible by
  three - with every level in the batch solver-verified before it ships.

CONTEXT
  tools/solver/generate.py
  tools/solver/schema.py (validate: acyclic blocked_by, ids unique, /3 counts)
  tools/tests/test_generate.py
  knowledge/solver/02-level-format-and-property-tests.md (Hypothesis, generate
    valid-by-construction rather than filter-and-reject)

SCOPE
  + `generate_level`: layered DAG so blocked_by cycles are impossible by
    construction, explicit optional `kind_count`.
  + `main()`: batch of 100 per run, unsolvable levels rejected and counted.
  + Property test: every generated level is solver-verified (the milestone's
    key test - one failure rejects M3).
  - Not which pile sizes to generate - that curve is 04-difficulty-curve.
  - Not room/pile_index assignment for shipping - that is 05-ship-37-levels.

OUTCOME
  `python -m tools.solver.generate --count 100 --out <dir>` produces a batch
  where every saved level parses and solves; rejects are reported, not shipped.

VERIFY (ARCH)
  1. `pytest tools/tests/test_generate.py -v` green, including
     test_batch_of_100_all_parse_all_solvable.
  2. Batch of 100 all parse in the loader (`schema.load_level`).
  3. `test_explicit_kind_count_is_respected` passes - kind_count is a real
     lever, not derived.

ROLE
  TOOLS, ARCH (review)

DEPENDS
  30-levels-solver/02-solver
