## Why this is the single largest risk in the project

art-brief.md section 7 opens by calling this "the task the executor can fail
outright" and asks to read that section more carefully than the others.
art-prompts.md section 0, rule 3 and section 4 repeat the same warning from
the generation side.

The acceptance criterion is deliberately handed to an outsider - "one cat, or
different cats?" - because, per art-brief.md section 7 and ROLES.md, an
executor (human or agent) almost always reports its own output as consistent.
Judgment belongs to someone with nothing invested in the answer.

**Two attempts, then hire an artist.** art-brief.md section 7 and
cat-shelter-tasks.md's M4 schedule both say this. Do not run a third
in-house/generation attempt hoping the third try fixes what two did not - the
brief is explicit that dragging this out costs more than accepting the
verdict and hiring a professional for a one-off. If the outsider says
"different cats" twice, close this task as blocked and open a new task to
commission an artist; do not reopen this one for a third generation pass.

## Masks are not generated by a model - hand-traced only

This applies to 40-art/04-cat-layers, not to this task's own output, but it
is recorded here because the two tasks are easy to conflate: someone reading
only this task.txt could reasonably assume the same pipeline produces the
masks. It does not.

art-prompts.md section 4 ("Что порождается нейросетью, а что нет") is
explicit: pattern masks and white-marking masks must align with the base
silhouette pixel for pixel, and no image model reproduces an existing image
exactly - it repaints the cat from scratch. Masks are made by hand-tracing
the already-accepted base silhouette in an image editor (outline the region,
fill white on black). This is manual work and must be budgeted as such, not
folded into a generation batch.

The same logic applies to room pairs in 40-art/07-rooms: generate the clean
room first, derive the dirty one by editing that same image (or generating
with it as a reference) - two independent prompts produce two different
rooms, which is exactly the failure mode section 5 of art-prompts.md warns
against for rooms.
