TASK: Six cat silhouettes

GOAL
  The desaturated base the coat shader paints at runtime: one cat, three
  states of neglect-to-care, two fur lengths. The single largest risk in
  the project - see NOTES.md.

CONTEXT
  art-brief.md section 7, subsections "Как кот устроен" and "Что рисуется"
  art-prompts.md section 4, subsections "Общая мысль" and "Наказы на шесть
  силуэтов"

SCOPE
  + 6 files: 3 states (hunched/thin, tidy/walking, sleek/resting) x 2 fur
    lengths (short, long).
  + Desaturated/greyscale only - colour is applied later by the shader.
  + Long-haired generated using the short-haired image as reference, not a
    fresh prompt - art-prompts.md section 4.
  - Not the pattern/marking/eye masks - see 40-art/04-cat-layers; NOT
    generated by any model at all, hand-traced, see NOTES.md.
  - No collar, clothing, accessories, human hands, other cats, food, bowl.
  - No colour decision of any kind - base_color/pattern/eye_color are
    runtime traits, not art choices.

OUTCOME
  6 PNGs, cat_<state>_<fur>_base.png, 512x512, transparent alpha, under
  game/Assets/Art.

VERIFY (HUMAN)
  1. All 6 files present, 512x512, greyscale (no hue in the fur).
  2. Outsider shown all 6, asked "is this one cat, or different cats?" -
     only "one cat" passes. Recorded verbatim in VERIFY.md.
  3. Same or a second outsider confirms the three states read worse-to-
     better unprompted.

ROLE
  ART, HUMAN

DEPENDS
  00-validate-demand/04-cat-two-states
