TASK: Cat layer masks

GOAL
  The black-and-white masks the coat shader reads to paint pattern, white
  markings and eye colour onto each of the 6 base silhouettes.

CONTEXT
  art-brief.md section 7, subsections "Layers for each silhouette" and
  "Mask requirements"
  art-prompts.md section 4, subsection "Layers made by hand"
  40-art/03-cat-silhouettes/NOTES.md (why hand-traced, not generated)

SCOPE
  + Per silhouette: 5 pattern masks (tabby, bicolor, calico, tuxedo,
    pointed), 3 marking masks (chest, paws, face), 1 eyes mask - 9 x 6 = 54
    files (solid needs none, the bare base is solid).
  + Hand-traced against the accepted base, pixel-for-pixel, same pose/frame.
  + White markings readable by outline+shadow on white fur, not fill alone.
  - Not the base silhouettes - see 40-art/03-cat-silhouettes, accepted first.
  - Not the runtime shader consuming these - VIEW work, excluded from this
    milestone.
  - No AI-generated mask attempt - a model cannot reproduce the base pixel
    for pixel, see 03-cat-silhouettes/NOTES.md.

OUTCOME
  54 mask PNGs (cat_<state>_<fur>_pattern_<name>.png / _mark_<name>.png /
  _eyes.png), 1024x1024, strict black/white with <=2px edge antialiasing,
  under game/Assets/Art. The size follows the base it is traced against -
  see 40-art/03, corrected 2026-08-27.

VERIFY (QA)
  1. Every mask pure black/white and does not extend past the base's alpha.
  2. Overlaying each mask on its base at 100% opacity: zero pixel offset.
  3. All 6 trait combinations composited by hand, checked by someone other
     than the mask author: 6 coherent cats, no drift, no floating marking.

ROLE
  ART, QA

DEPENDS
  40-art/03-cat-silhouettes
