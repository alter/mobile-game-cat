TASK: App icon, five variants

GOAL
  The single image a stranger judges before installing anything - decides
  more than the 12 levels do, per art-brief.md section 11.

CONTEXT
  art-brief.md section 10, subsection "Значок приложения"
  art-prompts.md section 6, subsection "Значок приложения, пять вариантов"

SCOPE
  + 5 compositions (face close-up, cat through doorway, cat behind box,
    split dirty/tidy, cat asleep on blanket), one shared prompt per variant.
  + Square, fills entire frame, no margins, no transparency, no rounded
    corners - system applies the mask.
  + A cat visible on all five.
  - Not "which is prettier" as a question - poll asks which one a person
    would tap.
  - No text, logo or watermark - icons are the asset most tempted to add a
    wordmark; restated here on top of the standing negative block.

OUTCOME
  5 PNGs, icon_<n>.png for n in 1..5, 1024x1024, no alpha, under
  game/Assets/Art.

VERIFY (HUMAN)
  1. All 5 files present, 1024x1024, fully opaque, no rounding baked in.
  2. Poll of 10 outsiders, each asked "which would you tap?" - not "which
     is prettiest." All 10 answers recorded in VERIFY.md, not just a tally.
  3. If the top two variants are within 1 vote, record that as inconclusive
     rather than declaring a winner.

ROLE
  ART, HUMAN

DEPENDS

  40-art/03-cat-silhouettes
