TASK: House map

GOAL
  The screen showing all 12 rooms and how much is left - new asset group,
  absent from the old M4 list; serves the "unfinished set nags" motive.

CONTEXT
  art-brief.md section 9 (file list, why a house cutaway, far-glance
  readability requirement)
  art-prompts.md section 6, subsection "House map" (cutaway prompt, the
  3-state rule: lightness carries the difference, not hue)

SCOPE
  + map_background.png: whole house, cutaway (more legible than a grid per
    art-brief.md section 9), empty rooms.
  + 3 state cells x 12 rooms = 36 files: dirty (dark), partial (half-lit,
    clear boundary), clean (warm, light) - lightness, not colour.
  - Not the room backgrounds - see 40-art/07-rooms; these are small map
    cells, not full-screen art, not derived pixel-for-pixel from the rooms.
  - No numbers, progress bars or text baked into the cells.
  - Not one cell per pile. Rooms hold 1-4 piles, so "partial" covers 25%, 50%
    and 75% alike; the map says which rooms are left, and the room itself
    shows how far through it you are. Exactness is available from
    RoomPlan.ClearedFractionAfter if it is ever wanted.

OUTCOME
  37 PNGs: map_background.png plus map_room_<nn>_<state>.png for nn 01..12,
  state in {dirty,partial,clean}, 256x256 per cell, under game/Assets/Art.

VERIFY (QA)
  1. 37 files present, cells 256x256, background sized per section 9.
  2. Converted to greyscale, the three states of any one room still order
     dark-to-light - proves the distinction survives on lightness alone.
  3. All 12 rooms' cells laid out together read as "mostly unfinished" or
     "mostly done" in one glance, checked by someone other than the author.

ROLE
  ART, QA

DEPENDS
  40-art/01-props
