TASK: Before/after share-card frame

GOAL
  The frame around the image a player shares - room before on the left, after
  on the right, cat in front. New asset, absent from the old M4 list.

CONTEXT
  art-brief.md section 10, subsection "\"Before — after\" card frame"
  art-prompts.md section 6, subsection "\"Before — after\" card frame"

SCOPE
  + share_frame.png: square frame split into two equal halves by a thin
    vertical line, light-oak moulding, both halves empty (the app composites
    room/cat images into it at share time, this task draws the frame only).
  + Transparent overlay, so it can sit on top of whatever image is composited
    behind it.
  - No space reserved for the cat's name - decided against putting a name on
    the public card, art-brief.md section 10.
  - Not the composited share image itself (room + cat placed inside the
    frame) - that is runtime/VIEW work in a later phase, not an art asset.

OUTCOME
  1 PNG, share_frame.png, 1080x1080, transparent alpha except the moulding,
  under game/Assets/Art.

VERIFY (QA)
  1. File present, 1080x1080, alpha transparent inside both halves.
  2. No name field, no text, no logo baked into the frame.
  3. A placeholder room image and a placeholder cat cut-out, dropped behind
     the frame, land inside the two halves without being cropped by the
     moulding.

ROLE
  ART, QA

DEPENDS
  40-art/01-props
