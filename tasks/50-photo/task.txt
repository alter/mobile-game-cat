TASK: Cat photo capture

GOAL
  Let the player photograph her own cat and see it in the game. This is where
  attachment forms (GOAL.md), and where metric 2, "uploaded a photo", fires.

CONTEXT
  cat-shelter-mvp.md section 5 (capture flow, why no image generation)
  cat-shelter-tech.md sections 3-4 (two-stage recognition, the Worker)
  DECISIONS.md D11 (request signing deleted, spend cap instead)
  ROLES.md (TOOLS owns /worker, NATIVE owns Plugins/iOS, VIEW owns screens)

SCOPE
  + 01: the 40-image fixture every other task measures against.
  + 02..04: the Cloudflare Worker - endpoint, strict-JSON schema, rate limit.
  + 05..07: on-device gate - Vision plugin, outcome copy, crop/downscale.
  + 08..10: capture screen, meet-your-cat screen, skip path.
  + 11: offline fallback.
  - Not the cat's art layers, silhouettes or coat shader - that is 40-art.
  - Not the booster or lose-screen economy - that is DECISIONS.md D4.
  - Not GameAnalytics event wiring for the funnel - that is 70-analytics.

OUTCOME
  A player can take or pick a photo, get accepted or rejected with the right
  message, and see a cat built from her own cat's traits - or skip to a
  default cat - all working with the Worker reachable or not.

VERIFY (ARCH)
  1. The reference set exists, checked against 01-reference-photo-set/task.txt.
  2. The Worker is deployed, 100% parseable in-enum traits on the reference set.
  3. The on-device gate produces four distinct outcomes on the reference set.
  4. A human photographs her own cat and recognises the result.

ROLE
  HUMAN, TOOLS, NATIVE, VIEW, ARCH (review)

DEPENDS

  60-shell-build/18-coat-shader
