TASK: Presentation, input, placement animation

GOAL
  Render one level with real art and let a player take an item from the pile
  and place it on the shelf, with the taking and placing animated.

CONTEXT
  cat-shelter-mvp.md section 3 (pile, shelf, matching)
  knowledge/unity/03-ui-toolkit-runtime.md (pointer events, drag-and-drop has
  no built-in component, must be built on PointerManipulator)

SCOPE
  + Render Board.GetAvailable() items as sprites; hidden items show the
    "unknown" prop art.
  + Drag from pile to shelf via PointerDownEvent/PointerMoveEvent/
    PointerUpEvent/PointerCaptureOutEvent, snap to nearest open slot.
  + Triple match plays a clear animation; shelf slot frees.
  − Not room-level composition (several piles, corners) - that is
    02-room-piles.
  − Not click sound or haptic feedback - that is 10-click-haptics.
  − Not save/resume - that is 08-mid-level-save.

OUTCOME
  One level, fully playable by touch, with placement and match animated.

VERIFY (QA)
  1. PlayMode: full level run, pile empties, shelf matches fire, level ends
     in Win.
  2. A covered item renders as the "unknown" prop, not its real sprite.
  3. Dropping an item outside any slot returns it to its pile position.

ROLE
  VIEW

DEPENDS
  20-rules-core/04-outcomes
  40-art
