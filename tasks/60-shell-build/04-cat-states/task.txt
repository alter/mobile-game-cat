TASK: Three cat states

GOAL
  The cat visibly changes twice across the game, tied to rooms finished, not
  to level numbers, so the arc survives if the pile count per room is later
  rebalanced.

CONTEXT
  cat-shelter-mvp.md section 4 (three states table: rooms 1-4 gaunt, 5-8
  tidier, 9-12 groomed)
  art-brief.md section 7 (how the cat is assembled from parts)

SCOPE
  + Cat.state derived from count of fully completed rooms: 1 for 1-4 done,
    2 for 5-8 done, 3 for 9-12 done.
  + Transition fires immediately after the 4th and 8th completed room, not
    at any fixed level number.
  + Transition swaps the cat's pose/behaviour set and is visible in the room
    the transition happens in.
  − Not reward props (bowl, blanket) - that is 05-rewards, even though both
    fire on the same two boundaries.
  − Not the photo-derived per-cat traits (colour, pattern) - that is
    50-photo; this task only switches state on top of whatever cat was built.

OUTCOME
  A cat whose appearance and behaviour change exactly twice, at completed
  rooms 4 and 8, regardless of how many levels those rooms took.

VERIFY (QA)
  1. PlayMode at both boundaries: state changes the instant the 4th and the
     8th room close, not before, not on a later level inside the next room.
  2. Changing a room's pile_count in level data does not shift when the
     transition fires - it still fires on room completion count.

ROLE
  VIEW

DEPENDS
  60-shell-build/02-room-piles
  40-art
