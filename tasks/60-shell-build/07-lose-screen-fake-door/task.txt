TASK: Lose screen - "one more shelf" fake door

GOAL
  Measure willingness to pay to be rescued from a jam, without granting the
  rescue. Metric 4 (tapped "one more shelf") is the only gate-3 signal that a
  screen in this milestone directly produces.

CONTEXT
  cat-shelter-mvp.md section 6 (the fake door, why the booster is not real,
  the four-booster measurement table)
  tasks/DECISIONS.md (booster: what capacity growth is for, once-per-level
  rule, the "one more shelf" naming)

SCOPE
  + Shown on GameOutcome.ShelfJammed.
  + Button reads "one more shelf" (not "+3 slots" or "+1 slot" - spreadsheet
    language does not belong in a game about tidying a home).
  + Tap is counted and fires an analytics hook (event name/shape left to
    70-analytics; this task only calls the hook).
  + Tapping shows a "coming soon" stub. The level stays lost. Replay is
    offered.
  − `Shelf.AddSlots` must NEVER be called from this screen or anywhere in the
    MVP. This is not an oversight to fix later - it is the point of the
    task. See NOTES.md for what happens to win rate if it is (72% -> 95%).
  − Not the eventual real booster (3 slots, once per level) - that ships
    post-MVP, after gate 3, per DECISIONS.md.

OUTCOME
  A lose screen that offers a credible rescue, records intent to pay, and
  never rescues anyone in this build.

VERIFY (QA)
  1. PlayMode: reaching ShelfJammed shows the screen; tapping the button
     shows the stub, replays the same (lost) level, and the analytics hook
     fires exactly once per tap.
  2. Grep across `Assets/Shell` and `Assets/View` for `AddSlots` - zero call
     sites outside of unit tests.

ROLE
