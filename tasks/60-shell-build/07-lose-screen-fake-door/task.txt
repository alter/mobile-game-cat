TASK: Lose screen - "one more shelf" fake door

GOAL
  Show a lose screen on a jam that tells the truth: a count of levels
  finished, and a way to try again - nothing offered that will not be
  granted.

  This was not always the goal. As built through 2026-08-27, this screen
  also carried a fake "one more shelf" offer meant to measure willingness
  to pay (metric 4) without granting the rescue. DECISIONS.md D4 was
  revised on 2026-08-27 and removed that offer entirely: a tap on a free
  button answers "do you want to not lose", which everyone does, and is not
  evidence anyone would pay. Both decisions are real and both are recorded
  in D4, not summarised here - read the whole entry, not just its ending.

CONTEXT
  cat-shelter-mvp.md section 4/6 (why a jam is not punishment, the tone
    rule for losing - a fact, not a reproach)
  tasks/DECISIONS.md D4, the whole entry, including "Revised 2026-08-27:
    the door is closed until there is a price behind it"
  tasks/80-live-validation/00-thresholds/NOTES.md, "Metric four lost its
    instrument, 2026-08-27" - what D4's revision costs downstream, and the
    three options on record for replacing the instrument before any
    monetisation decision. This task does not solve that; it only must not
    be read as if the instrument still exists.

SCOPE
  + Shown on GameOutcome.ShelfJammed.
  + Shows a count of levels finished so far ("Levels finished: {0}.") and a
    Replay button that restarts the same lost level. No blame, no counting
    of days or attempts missed beyond that one number - a fact, not a
    reproach.
  − No offer, no booster button, no "coming soon" stub. These existed from
    2026-08-25 to 2026-08-27 (the task as originally written, below) and
    were removed by DECISIONS.md D4's revision.
  − `Shelf.AddSlots` and `Board.AddShelfSlots` must NEVER be called from
    this screen or anywhere in the MVP. `Analytics.BoosterTap` and
    `Board.AddShelfSlots` both stay in `Core` - declared, tested, and
    unreachable (`tools/tests/test_analytics_call_sites.py`'s `DORMANT`
    entry pins this down and fails if either silently disappears or
    silently starts firing) - ready for when a real, priced booster ships.
  − Not the eventual real booster (3 slots, once per level, priced) - that
    ships post-MVP, after gate 3, per DECISIONS.md D4.
  − Not a replacement instrument for metric 4. Dropping it from gate 3,
    pricing the booster for real, or asking publishers instead of
    measuring - that choice belongs to `tasks/80-live-validation/00-thresholds`,
    not here.

OUTCOME
  A lose screen that states a fact (levels finished) and offers only a
  replay - no rescue, real or fake, and no analytics event fires from it.
  Metric 4 has no instrument in this build; that gap is tracked, not
  solved, by `tasks/80-live-validation/00-thresholds`.

VERIFY (QA)
  1. PlayMode: reaching ShelfJammed shows the screen with the
     levels-finished count and a Replay button only - no second button, no
     stub. Tapping Replay restarts the same (lost) level.
  2. Grep across `Assets/Shell` and `Assets/View` for `AddSlots` - zero
     call sites outside of unit tests.
  3. Grep across `Assets/Shell` and `Assets/View` for `Analytics.BoosterTap`
     - zero real call sites (comments explaining why it is dormant do not
     count - see the note on that distinction in NOTES.md).
     `tools/tests/test_analytics_call_sites.py` passes, confirming
     `booster:tap` stays declared and dormant rather than either silently
     firing or silently disappearing.

ROLE
  VIEW
