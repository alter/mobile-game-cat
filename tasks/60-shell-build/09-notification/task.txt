TASK: Notification, permission after level 2

GOAL
  One local notification per day, fired if the player hasn't returned. This
  is the only mechanism in the entire MVP designed to drive metric 3
  (day-1 return), which is one of the three go/no-go gates.

CONTEXT
  cat-shelter-mvp.md section 4 (soft, no-guilt copy: "Мурзик нашёл что-то за
  диваном" -> in English, no naming/shaming tone)
  knowledge/ios/05-notifications-permissions.md (AuthorizationRequest,
  iOSNotificationCalendarTrigger for a daily evening slot, the 64-pending
  limit, and why the "asking first doubles refusal" claim does not survive
  its own citation chain)
  tasks/DECISIONS.md

SCOPE
  + Permission requested after the player completes level 2, not on first
    launch.
  + com.unity.mobile.notifications: AuthorizationRequest, then
    iOSNotificationCalendarTrigger for one evening slot per day, Repeats=true.
  + Soft, blame-free copy in English.
  − Do not cite or repeat any specific opt-in percentage tied to request
    timing (e.g. "doubles the refusal rate") in code comments, copy, or
    reports - the sources for that number don't hold up. See NOTES.md.
  − Not provisional authorization (silent, no banner/sound) - the goal here
    is a noticeable return prompt, not an unobtrusive one.
  − Not ATT / IDFA tracking - this game does not request it at all.

OUTCOME
  A build that asks for notification permission once, after level 2, and (if
  granted) reliably fires one evening notification per day of inactivity.

VERIFY (NATIVE)
  1. On device: complete level 2, confirm the system permission dialog
     appears at that point and not before.
  2. On device: grant permission, background the app, confirm a notification
     fires that evening and again 24h later without the app being reopened.
  3. Copy reviewed against cat-shelter-mvp.md section 4's tone requirement:
     no guilt, no urgency language.

ROLE
  NATIVE
