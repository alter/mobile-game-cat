TASK: Click and haptics on placement

GOAL
  Give placing an item on the shelf a satisfying click sound and haptic tap,
  the small per-move feedback that makes 10-20 minutes of play feel good
  rather than mechanical.

CONTEXT
  cat-shelter-mvp.md section 3 (the tactile "tidying" appeal the genre is
  built on)

SCOPE
  + A short click/thunk sound on successful placement.
  + A light haptic pulse on successful placement (iOS haptics API).
  + A distinct (stronger or different) cue on a triple match.
  − Not sound/haptics on failed placement or on the lose screen tap - keep
    negative moments quiet, this task is about the satisfying ones.
  − Not a settings toggle for sound/haptics - out of scope for the MVP.

OUTCOME
  Every successful placement and every match has an audible and tactile
  response.

VERIFY (HUMAN)
  1. A person plays 5 minutes straight on device and confirms the feedback
     feels present and pleasant, not absent or annoying, without being
     prompted about what to listen or feel for.

ROLE
  VIEW, HUMAN

DEPENDS
  60-shell-build/01-presentation-input
