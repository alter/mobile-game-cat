TASK: TestFlight distribution

GOAL
  Get the .ipa in front of real testers on real devices, the first check
  that anything built in this milestone actually runs outside the dev
  machine.

CONTEXT
  knowledge/ios/01-appstore-requirements-2026.md (internal testers: up to
  100, builds available 90 days; external testers: up to 10,000 per app but
  require an internal group first and go through Beta App Review; PrivacyInfo
  manifest and the camera-usage App Privacy declaration are required before
  any build is accepted)

SCOPE
  + Upload the .ipa from 13-headless-build to App Store Connect.
  + Create an internal testing group, invite at least 3 people.
  + Complete the App Privacy ("nutrition label") declaration for photo
    collection - required because the cropped photo is shared with a
    third-party processor (Anthropic) for trait extraction and retained
    there for up to 30 days by default (not indefinitely, not used for
    training - corrected 2026-08-27, sourced in
    tasks/00-validate-demand/01-market-scan/legal-risk.md §3). That is
    still "collection" under Apple's App Privacy rules regardless of
    retention length; the declaration must not say "not stored."
  − Not external/public TestFlight testers or Beta App Review - internal
    testing only for this milestone.
  − Not the App Store Connect record/tax/banking setup itself - that is
    10-accounts.

OUTCOME
  A build installable by invited testers directly from TestFlight.

VERIFY (NATIVE)
  1. At least 3 invited people confirm the app installed and launched from
     the TestFlight invite on their own devices.
  2. App Store Connect shows the build as processed with the App Privacy
     declaration filled in, not pending/incomplete.

ROLE
  NATIVE

DEPENDS
  60-shell-build/13-headless-build
  10-accounts/02-apple-developer-program
  10-accounts/05-app-store-connect-record
