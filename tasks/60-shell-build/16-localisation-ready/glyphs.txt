A font built for this game must cover the COMBINED line. The per-language
lines are here so a subset font can be built per store listing, and so a
missing glyph can be traced to the table that asked for it.

Included on purpose and easy to forget: U+0020 space, the ASCII of
"Sootpaw", the braces and digit of {0} (string.Format leaves them on
screen when a call site passes no argument), the horizontal ellipsis
U+2026, the middle dot U+00B7 that separates map.legend's clauses, the
em dash U+2014, and every combining mark — Thai vowels and tone marks,
Arabic shadda and hamza, Devanagari matras and the nukta U+093C.
