TASK: Audit what the app asks permission for, and cut it to the minimum

GOAL
  Every permission and every capability in the shipped Info.plist is there
  because something the player does needs it, with the reason written down.
  Anything a package dragged in unasked is removed.

CONTEXT
  knowledge/ios/05-notifications-permissions.md (notifications, ATT)
  knowledge/ios/01-appstore-requirements-2026.md (privacy manifest, review)
  tasks/DECISIONS.md D9 (ATT is deliberately never requested)
  game/build/ios/CatShelter/Info.plist (what is actually generated today)

SCOPE
  + An inventory of every Ns*UsageDescription, UIBackgroundModes entry,
    entitlement and capability in the generated Info.plist, each with: who put
    it there, what player-visible action needs it, and what breaks without it.
  + Remove or disable everything without an answer to the second column.
  + Usage-description strings written for a person, not for review: "to find
    your cat in the photo" beats "This app requires camera access".
  + Check what the packages add unasked - purchasing, analytics, notifications
    and the vision plugin each can contribute keys.
  + The privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) checked against what the app
    actually collects, since a wrong one is a review rejection.
  - Not ATT: D9 settles it. Never call RequestTrackingAuthorization; call
    EnableAdvertisingIdTracking(false) before Initialize(). This task only
    confirms no package quietly reintroduces it.
  - Not the photo permission flow itself - that is 50-photo/08-capture-screen.

OUTCOME
  A table in this task's NOTES.md listing every permission with its
  justification, and a build whose Info.plist contains nothing beyond it.

VERIFY (NATIVE)
  1. `plutil -p` on the shipped Info.plist: every Ns*UsageDescription and
     UIBackgroundModes entry appears in the table with a reason.
  2. A launch on the simulator raises no permission dialog the player has not
     asked for by an action.
  3. `grep` for RequestTrackingAuthorization across Assets and Packages: no
     call site (D9).

ROLE
  NATIVE

DEPENDS
  50-photo/05-vision-plugin
  60-shell-build/09-notification
