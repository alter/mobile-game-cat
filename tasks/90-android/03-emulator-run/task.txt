TASK: The game runs on an Android emulator

GOAL
  A screenshot of the real board on Android. Until something is installed and
  looked at, "it builds" means nothing - on iOS a build that compiled cleanly
  still showed a black screen, twice.

CONTEXT
  90-android/02-build-pipeline (the .apk this installs)
  60-shell-build/13-headless-build/NOTES.md (the iOS equivalent, and the
    simulator commands that shape these)

SCOPE
  + An AVD created from the command line, named and recorded, so a second
    person makes the same one.
  + adb install, launch, screenshot - all scriptable, no Android Studio.
  + The 37 levels load and the first board is playable: tiles respond, a
    triple matches, the shelf fills.
  + The commands written into cat-shelter-tech.md section 5 beside the iOS
    ones.
  - Not a physical device - that is 14-device-matrix.
  - Not performance work. Slow on an emulator is not slow on a phone.
  - Not the photo flow: the emulator has no camera worth testing and ML Kit
    behaviour there is 01's problem.

OUTCOME
  A screenshot of level 1 running on the emulator, filed in this directory,
  plus the exact command sequence that produced it.

VERIFY (NATIVE)
  1. `adb shell pm list packages` shows the app installed.
  2. The screenshot shows the board with 36 tiles and a nine-slot shelf.
  3. Taking three tiles of one kind removes them - shown by a second
     screenshot or by the save file's contents.

ROLE
  NATIVE

DEPENDS
  90-android/02-build-pipeline
