TASK: Android crop and downscale

GOAL
  The photo reaching the Worker from Android is the same shape as from iOS:
  512x512, under 200 KB. Otherwise the model sees two different products and
  the traits differ by platform for no reason the player caused.

CONTEXT
  game/Assets/Shell/CatPhoto.cs - the facade and the two constants
  game/Assets/Plugins/iOS/CatPhoto.swift - the iOS half, and the reasoning
    that matters more than the code: 512 because image cost is
    ceil(w/28)*ceil(h/28) tokens, 200 px because accuracy falls off below it,
    and the widen-rather-than-upscale rule
  50-photo/07-crop-downscale/NOTES.md - the measured result: the under-200px
    guard fired on 12 of 27 real boxes, so it is not a corner case

SCOPE
  + Crop to the recognition box, widen it when either side is under 200 px,
    square it off, scale to 512x512, JPEG under 200 KB with stepped quality.
  + Same behaviour as iOS on the same inputs - not "similar".
  + Run over the crops of the reference set and compare sizes with the iOS
    run: every output 512x512 and under the cap.
  - Not orientation handling - that belongs to 05, as it does on iOS.
  - Not a shared C# implementation. Decoding and re-encoding a JPEG in
    managed code costs more than the platform call and would have to be
    measured before being worth it.

OUTCOME
  CatPhoto.Prepare returns a 512x512 JPEG under 200 KB on Android, byte sizes
  filed beside the iOS ones.

VERIFY (ARCH)
  1. Every accepted reference image processed on Android: all 512x512, all
     under 200 KB, counts recorded.
  2. The widen guard fires on the same images it fired on for iOS, or the
     difference is explained.
  3. Five crops eyeballed - the cat is whole and centred, not clipped.

ROLE
  NATIVE

DEPENDS
  90-android/05-recognition-plugin
