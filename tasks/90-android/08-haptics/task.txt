TASK: Android click and haptics

GOAL
  Placing an item feels the same on Android: a short click and a light tap.
  This is the per-move feedback that makes ten minutes of tidying pleasant
  rather than mechanical, and it is the only thing the player feels hundreds
  of times per session.

CONTEXT
  game/Assets/Shell/Feedback.cs - Place() and Match(), the two call sites,
    and the synthesised blips that stand in until audio assets exist
  game/Assets/Plugins/iOS/CatHaptics.swift - the iOS half, and why the
    generators are kept prepared: one created at the moment of the tap arrives
    late enough to feel unrelated to it
  60-shell-build/10-click-haptics/task.txt - the scope rule that only the
    good moments get feedback

SCOPE
  + VibrationEffect through AndroidJavaObject: a light predefined click for a
    placement, something distinctly stronger for a match.
  + API-level fallback: predefined effects need API 29+, so older devices get
    a short one-shot rather than nothing.
  + Respect the system's touch-feedback setting rather than vibrating over it.
  - Not sound. The synthesised blips are already platform-neutral; they play
    on Android unchanged.
  - Not feedback on a refused tap or on the lose screen, on either platform.
  - Not VIBRATE-permission gymnastics: it is a normal permission, granted at
    install, and it goes in the audit table like every other one.

OUTCOME
  Feedback.Place and Feedback.Match produce a distinguishable vibration on
  Android, degrading to a short buzz below API 29 and to silence where the
  device has no vibrator.

VERIFY (HUMAN)
  1. On a physical device: a placement and a match feel different, judged by
     someone not told which is which.
  2. With system haptics turned off, the game does not vibrate.
  3. On an API 28 device or emulator image, the app does not crash and still
     buzzes.

ROLE
  NATIVE, HUMAN

DEPENDS
  90-android/03-emulator-run
