TASK: Google Play developer account

GOAL
  The account that lets an Android build reach a tester. Nothing downstream of
  it can be checked without it, and it takes days that do not shrink with
  effort - so it starts early or it blocks the end of the phase.

CONTEXT
  10-accounts/02-apple-developer-program - the iOS equivalent and what it
    blocked
  DECISIONS.md D11 (spend caps are the real protection; this is a one-off fee,
    not a running cost)

SCOPE
  + A Google Play developer account, one-off registration fee, in Active
    state.
  + Identity verification completed - for individual accounts this is the step
    that actually takes time, and it cannot be hurried.
  + An app record created with the package name the build already uses, so the
    two do not diverge.
  + The fee and the date recorded, so the project's real spend stays known.
  - Not the store listing, screenshots or description.
  - Not billing or payouts: the MVP sells nothing. That is a later task if the
    game survives gate 3.
  - Not closed or open testing tracks - internal testing only, in 13.

OUTCOME
  A Play Console account in Active state with an app record whose package name
  matches the build, and the fee recorded.

VERIFY (HUMAN)
  1. The console shows the account active and the app record created.
  2. The package name in the console matches `aapt dump badging` on the .aab.
  3. The amount and date are written down next to the Apple fee.

ROLE
  HUMAN

DEPENDS
  90-android/00-android-decision
