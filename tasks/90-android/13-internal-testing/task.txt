TASK: A tester installs the build from Play internal testing

GOAL
  The Android equivalent of TestFlight: a build a real person installs on a
  real phone from the store, not a sideloaded apk. Everything the emulator
  cannot answer - vibration, camera, ML Kit on a real chip, notifications
  surviving a night - waits on this.

CONTEXT
  60-shell-build/14-testflight - the iOS equivalent, still open for the same
    reason (no account)
  90-android/02-build-pipeline (the .aab this uploads)
  90-android/10-permission-audit (the Data Safety answers this needs)

SCOPE
  + An .aab uploaded to the internal testing track, signed by Play App
    Signing.
  + The Data Safety form filled from the permission audit's table, not
    independently - two different answers to the same question is how a review
    rejection happens.
  + At least one tester on the list who is not the author.
  + The install link recorded here.
  - Not closed testing, open testing or production.
  - Not the twelve-tester-two-week requirement for personal accounts going to
    production: internal testing does not need it, and production is not in
    this phase.
  - Not a store listing beyond the minimum internal testing demands.

OUTCOME
  A build installable from Play internal testing by an invited tester, with
  the link and the tester's confirmation filed here.

VERIFY (HUMAN)
  1. A tester who is not the author installs from the link and reaches level
     3.
  2. That tester photographs a cat and reports which of the four outcomes they
     saw.
  3. The Data Safety answers match the audit table row for row.

ROLE
  NATIVE, HUMAN

DEPENDS
  90-android/12-play-console
  90-android/10-permission-audit
